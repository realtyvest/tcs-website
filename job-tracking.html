What You Get From Tracking Every Job

Stop chasing crew updates via text and email. Job tracking in TCS keeps one record per job. The crew marks the map and submits, so the record shows what was done without adding busywork to your field teams.

One Record per Job

Every job gets one record, from the work order to the invoice. The crew's marked-up map and the tally from its marks land on that record, so the field and the office work from the same page.

Field App Workflow

The crew marks the map in TCS Map Annotator, placing each code and quantity where the work was done. The tally comes from the marks.

Submitted, and When

The office job list shows, for each job, how many times its map has been submitted and when the last submission came in.

Submitted From the Field

Crews mark the map and submit once from the field. The marked-up map and its tally land on the job record, so nobody calls to ask what was done.

Corrections Keep the History

When a job needs a correction, the crew resubmits it. The new version is the one that bills, and the earlier submission stays on the record as superseded. A job can have only one invoice at a time unless that invoice is voided.

Ready to Invoice

When the job is submitted, its tally is ready to invoice. The office drafts the invoice from it, each line priced from the rate card for that ISP or prime.

How Tracking Works, From Map to Invoice

The workflow is straightforward and requires minimal crew onboarding. Here's how it flows:

Job Setup

Each job gets one record in TCS, with the map the crew will mark and the rate codes it will be billed on. The job number and PO are entered once, when the job is set up.

Crew Opens the Map

The crew signs in on a phone and opens the job's map, the same map the office set up.

Mark the Map

On site, the crew places each code and quantity on the map where the work was done. The tally comes from the marks, not from a count typed in later.

Submit From the Field

At job end, the crew submits the marked map once from the field. Each submission is saved with who submitted it and when.

Billing Handoff

The office drafts the invoice from the submitted tally and reviews it before issuing. The invoice can go out the day the job is submitted.

Invoice Management

Every invoice, draft or issued, in one list.
