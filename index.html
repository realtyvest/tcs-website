The Field Is the Source of Truth

Your field knows what happened. Your office shouldn’t have to reconstruct it. Most ISP contractors don’t lose money because crews don’t work. They lose it in the handoffs: WhatsApp, phone photos, paper, one spreadsheet, then another, then accounting, until Friday is a rebuild of Tuesday.

TCS does not start with another office dashboard. It starts where the work happens: the map, the codes, the photos, the closeout, so production and billing inherit the same truth the crew already lived.

Capture the work once in the field. Move production, photos, closeouts, job cost, and billing through one connected workflow, so the office isn’t rebuilding Tuesday’s job on Friday.

How a job moves

One clean line from the field to the invoice. No Friday rebuild.

  1. 1 Assigned job lands with the crew
  2. 2 Mark the map codes and qty where the work happens
  3. 3 Submit once, from the field
  4. 4 Tally = marks what was produced, not a Friday rebuild
  5. 5 Invoice same day Ready to bill. Invoice the same day.
