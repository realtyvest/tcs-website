Questions

See it on one of your maps.

What does Map Annotator do?

It lets your crew mark billing codes and quantities directly on the as-built map, then gives the office an organized tally tied to those marks.

Can we buy it without Job Tracker?

Yes. Map Annotator is a standalone software product. Job Tracker is an optional next step, not a requirement.

Does it replace the original map?

No. The original map stays as the job background. The software adds clear, traceable annotations on top of it.

What should we bring to the demo?

Bring one representative map, the billing codes used on that work, and the way the office receives the completed as-built today.
