How Dig Documentation Works

Pre-Bore Setup

Before bore operations begin, crews photograph the site from multiple angles, document utility locate marks, record equipment positioning, and note 811 ticket status. Every pre-bore image carries GPS coordinates and precise timestamps.

Locate Mark Verification

Crews verify and photograph utility locate marks on site. Marks are recorded by utility type, mark color, crew who placed the mark, and verification status. GPS location of each mark is captured automatically.

Bore Path Documentation

As boring begins, crews document bore machine location, bore angles, equipment setup, and bore start point with GPS accuracy. Photos during boring show equipment positioning and bore path progression.

Real-Time Incident Capture

If damage occurs, crews immediately photograph the damage scene, record utility contact information, capture crew statements, and document incident circumstances. All photos are timestamped and GPS-tagged in real-time.

Claim Documentation

The platform compiles pre-bore photos, locate marks, bore documentation, and damage photos into a complete damage claim package. Claim numbers are tracked, insurance correspondence is logged, and utility notifications are documented.

Automated Evidence Export

Generate comprehensive claim reports with all evidence organized chronologically. GPS data, timestamps, crew information, and incident details are formatted for claims adjusters and legal review. Complete claim packages are ready in minutes.
