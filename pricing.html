Terms

Drop Production and Map-to-Cash are month-to-month. Cancel with 30 days' written notice; you keep access through the paid period, and there are no prorated refunds. Full Production Operations, Construction Financial Control and Prime are 12-month terms that renew automatically for 12 months unless you give 60 days' notice before renewal. Autopay is required. Implementation is billed separately. Your full terms are in your Order Form.
