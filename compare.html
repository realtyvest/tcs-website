Why It Matters

Telecom is Not HVAC

ServiceTitan and Jobber are great products built for home service businesses. But telecom subcontracting has different workflows: owner billing formats, pre-bore documentation, 811 ticket tracking, multi-crew job costing, and ISP compliance requirements. Generic software forces you to build workarounds for every one of those.

Enterprise vs. Right-Sized

Praxedo and FieldSquared serve large enterprise fleets. Their pricing, implementation timelines, and feature sets are designed for operations with hundreds of technicians and dedicated IT teams. If you run 10 to 100 crews, you end up paying for features you do not use and waiting months to go live.

Your Crews Have to Actually Use It

The most capable software in the world does not help if your crews will not adopt it. TCS includes 90 days of hands-on onboarding with your crew leads. We do not hand you a login and wish you luck. We are on-site and on-call until every crew is running the system daily.
