How It Works

It starts with a free 30-minute fit call where we get a feel for your operation and decide together if a paid Operations Audit is the right next step. The Audit is two weeks of recorded interviews with ownership and crew, process mapping, an opportunity matrix, a prioritized roadmap, and ROI math on every recommendation. From there, we build whatever the Audit prescribes and stay through 90 days of adoption support after launch.

The first module is typically live within 2 to 4 weeks. We do not spend months on requirements documents. We build fast, get feedback from your crews in the field, and iterate.

We keep interfaces simple, reduce data entry to the minimum, and handle onboarding directly with your crew leads.

Pricing and Investment

There is a one-time implementation fee that covers system design, build-out, data migration, and crew onboarding. The exact amount depends on what the Operations Audit prescribes. We scope and quote everything on the fit call before you commit, so there are no surprises.

Most clients see returns within the first 60 days. The biggest wins are recovering unbilled work, cutting billing cycle time, and getting admin hours back. Use the ROI calculator to run your own numbers, or we walk it on the Fit Call.

We offer month-to-month and annual agreements. Annual agreements include a discount. There are no multi-year lock-ins.
