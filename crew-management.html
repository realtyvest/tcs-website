Everything You Need to Manage Your Workforce

Managing crews is more than assigning jobs. You need to know who's certified, who's insured, which vehicles they operate, and whether their contractor invoices match the work done. Crew management consolidates all that data and alerts you when action is needed.

Crew Roster

Add crew members with phone, email, certifications, and ratings. Mark who can drive which vehicles, operate specialized equipment, or work on specific job types. Sort by skill, availability, or location.

Compliance Documents

Store certificates of insurance (COI), DWC83 forms, W9s, driver's licenses, and any other required paperwork. Upload documents directly, set expiration dates, and the system alerts you when renewal is due.

Equipment & Vehicle Assignment

Track which crew operates which vehicles or equipment. Link tools, trucks, directional boring rigs, or aerial lifts to crew members. Know who's available and what assets they can deploy.

Contractor Invoice Management

If you work with contractors, store their invoices and mark them paid or disputed. Link invoices to jobs for reconciliation. See which contractors are owed money and how much work they've completed this period.

Expiration Alerts

The system monitors all expiration dates. Get notified 30, 14, and 7 days before insurance, certifications, or licenses expire. Never let compliance lapse because paperwork slipped your mind.

Crew Work History

See every job each crew member has worked on. Review their labor hours, completion rates, quality ratings, and customer feedback. Make smarter decisions about crew assignments and performance.

Managing Your Crew with TCS

Crew management is about knowing your team and staying on top of compliance. Here's how the workflow keeps everything organized.

1. Crew Entry

Add crew members to the system. Enter name, contact info, certifications, experience level, and any special skills. Assign them to teams or regions.

2. Document Upload

Upload all required compliance documents: insurance, certifications, tax forms. The system stores them with timestamps and flags expiration dates.

3. Compliance Monitoring

The system watches expiration dates. You get alerts well before expiry so there's time to request renewals from crew members or contractors.

4. Equipment Assignment

Link vehicles and equipment to crew members. Specify which assets each crew can operate or is responsible for maintaining.

5. Job Assignment

When you assign a job, you can filter crews by certification, location, vehicle availability, and current workload. Assign the right crew based on qualifications.

6. Performance Tracking

As jobs complete, build a history of each crew's work: hours logged, quality ratings, customer feedback. Use this data to optimize future assignments.

Compliance Is Peace of Mind

ISPs require proof of insurance and certifications. Missing documentation can halt a job or, worse, expose you to liability. By centralizing crew data and automating compliance monitoring, you eliminate gaps and prove to ISPs that your team is properly credentialed and insured.
