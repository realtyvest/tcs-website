Privacy Policy. Telecom Contractor Solutions (TCS)

This Privacy Policy explains how TCS handles personal information when you use our websites and Services. “TCS,” “we” and “us” mean Telecom Contractor Solutions LLC, a Texas limited liability company that also does business as Simple Contractor Systems. For jobs, maps, crew, and homeowner data inside a customer organization, that contractor is the controller and TCS is the processor. This Policy also covers information TCS controls (for example billing contacts, acceptance records and site analytics).

Who this applies to

  • Visitors to our marketing site
  • Account Owners and office users
  • Field users (Annotator) rostered by a customer
  • US-only offering

Information we process

  • Account and contact details (name, email)
  • Billing and subscription records
  • Job, map, tally, and invoice data Customer enters (processor)
  • Field login: your company’s organization code (a shared sign-in code, readable to your employer’s account owner, admins and project managers) and the last four digits of a roster phone number (stored only as a one-way keyed hash)
  • Crew start points: if your employer uses route planning, the usual starting point the office sets for each crew (a map point, with an optional label or address). We use it only to plan routes. We never use phone GPS to set it.
  • Technical logs, device/browser data, and security events
  • Terms acceptance records: user ID, organization, version accepted, date and time, IP address and device type
  • Marketing attribution details when you visit the site or request contact, including the landing page, source page, referring site, campaign tags, advertising click identifiers, and the link you selected
  • De-identified aggregates for product and industry stats (see below)

Field sign-in

If your organization turns on field login for Map Annotator, we process an organization code and the last four digits of a phone number your employer places on its crew roster. Signing in takes both the organization code and those four digits. We store the four digits only as a one-way keyed hash, not in readable form. The organization code is a shared company code, not a personal password, but it is part of how you sign in. We keep it in readable form so your employer’s account owner, and the admins and project managers your employer designates, can see and change it. When your employer changes the code, the old code may remain in our backups for a limited time until they expire. Field sign-in is not used for account-owner, billing, or admin access, and it is not how we verify a formal privacy request; those use your email / office login. Your employer controls the roster and when codes are rotated or revoked.

If you work on a job

If you add photos, signatures, locations or other records to a job, those records belong to the company that assigned the work. That company decides how long to keep them. If you ask us to delete your personal information, we will handle your own profile under this Policy, and we will refer requests about job records to that company. Your request does not delete the company's job records.

How we use information

To provide and secure the Services, authenticate users, bill Customer, record Terms acceptance, improve the product, communicate service messages, and comply with law. When you request a Fit Call or product demo, we use marketing attribution details to understand which page or campaign led to the request and to follow up about the request. Some product features may use automated processing, including AI-assisted extraction, to suggest data for a user to review. We may use and license de-identified aggregated marker/billing-code/production statistics; we strip identity; privacy requests do not unwind those aggregates.

Cookies, analytics, and embeds

Our marketing site uses Google Tag Manager (including a server-side tag endpoint on our domain) to load analytics tags, and may embed Google Calendar Appointments on the Fit Call page. See our Cookie Policy for categories and choices.

Sharing

We use subprocessors (for example hosting, analytics, form automation, email, and payments) under contracts. If you submit the Fit Call form, we send your name, company, email, phone, crew-size range, optional message and its marketing attribution details through an automation provider (Make.com) so our team can follow up. You can ask for our current subprocessor list at the email below. We do not sell personal information. We may disclose information if required by law or to protect rights and security. Each customer's data is kept logically separate from other customers' data.

Retention

Compliance records related to privacy requests (receipts / register / journal) are retained 3 years, then purged. Certain security tombstones used to prevent replay are kept permanently in pseudonymous form. Terms acceptance records are kept as long as needed to show what was agreed and as the law allows, even after an account closes or a deletion request.

Crew start points are handled differently. We keep a crew's current start point while the crew is active. Earlier start points, and start points stored with past routes, are deleted 30 days after they are replaced or after the route's service date passes, or sooner if your employer clears them. We do not use start points to track anyone's movements.

Business data retention follows Customer’s account and our Order Form / cancelation terms.

Your choices and requests

  • Commercial export (Customer): after cancel, paid-up accounts may receive data (not photos) for 60 days (Gil 2026-10-23 11:53 AM CT), on request.
  • Statutory profile requests: email us from the email on the Account; we verify by control of the account email. Unpaid invoices do not block a statutory profile request. Personal requests do not erase the organization’s job/business records.
  • Requests about someone else’s data go to a verified review process, not self-serve erase of another person.
  • TCS does not currently offer in-product tools to delete or export personal information. Send requests by email to the address below.

State privacy rights

Depending on where you live (for example Texas or California), you may have the right to access, correct, delete or get a copy of your personal information, and to opt out of the sale of personal information, targeted advertising or certain profiling. We do not sell personal information. To make a request, email us at the address below. If we decline your request, you may appeal by replying to our decision with "Appeal" in the subject line; we will respond as required by law. We will not discriminate against you for using these rights.

US-only / children

Services are for US business users. Not directed at children under 13.

Governing law and disputes

Texas law. Disputes between TCS and a customer are resolved as Section 15 of the Terms of Service says: TCS's collection of amounts owed goes to the courts in Harris County, Texas, and every other dispute goes to binding individual arbitration in Harris County, Texas.
